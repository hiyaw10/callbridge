Text Message Consent

This page shows the exact notice callers see before a business that uses CallBridge texts them, the messages they can expect, and how to stop them. {{ LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME }} operates CallBridge.

The consent notice

Every business that uses CallBridge posts this notice where customers find its phone number, such as its website, its Google Business listing, and its voicemail greeting:

Text message notice: By calling this number you agree that if we miss your call, we may text you back at the number you called from about your call and how we can help. This is a reply to your own call. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of buying anything. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

The agreement to receive texts is separate from the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, which are linked for reference only.

What text messages you will receive

  • A reply to a missed call. One text sent only to a number that just called the business and was not answered. Example: "Sorry we missed you — this is Acme Plumbing, how can we help?"
  • A review request. Sent only after the business marks your job complete, to the number you gave the business for that job.
  • Updates from the business. Sent only to customers who have agreed to receive them from that business.

We do not send marketing to purchased, rented, or scraped lists, and we never text a number that has not contacted the business or been given to it by the customer.

How often

Message frequency varies. A missed call produces one reply text. Further texts happen only if you reply and the business answers you.

Stopping messages and getting help

  • Reply STOP to any message to opt out. No further texts will be sent to your number from that business.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at support@callbridgeapp.com or +1 (833) 588-6479.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your information

We do not share mobile phone numbers, text message opt-in data, or consent information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Read more in our Privacy Policy and Opt In & Consent Policy.
